What is Bottoms Up!?

Bottoms Up! is a silly online party game. You and up to three friends sit on the rim of an inflatable raft and race it through canals, spikes and rapids, steering with nothing but farts. There's nothing to download and no account to make: type a name, share a 4-digit room code, and you're on the water.

How to play

Everyone faces the centre of the raft, so a fart pushes the raft from where you sit straight through its middle. To go forward, the people at the back fart. To steer, walk around the rim, or turn away from the centre to spin the raft. Push against each other and nothing happens: the skill is farting together at the right moment.

The rim is split evenly between whoever's playing: alone you get all 360°, two players get half each, three get 120° each, and four get a quarter. Gas runs out if you hold too long, so save some for the corners.

Levels

Pick any level you like, alone or with friends.

1. The Bendy Canal

A walled canal with a tight gap, a sharp turn onto spikes and a current that drags you sideways.

2. The Plank Road

No walls. Thin water roads high above a valley, with a crosswind and a sweeping gate. Drift off the edge and you fall.

3. Rapids Run

The long one: a spiked barrier, raging corners that are far easier with two people pushing, and a long unprotected road over the drop.

4. Windmill Ridge

No walls, and three giant windmills with spikes hanging down to the water. Ride round in the gap between the arms.

5. Hell's Wells

Glowing holes in the middle of the track that pull you in as you get close. Then a crosswind joins in.

6. The Inferno

Everything at once on a river that never stops pushing you off the edge: spike circles with two openings each, wells and a windmill.

Scoring and leaderboards

Only time counts; splats don't. Points are 1000 × par time ÷ your time, so finishing in par time scores 1000 and twice as fast scores 2000. There's a separate leaderboard for solo runs and for 2, 3 and 4-player crews.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no download and no account.

How many people can play?

One to four. Play single player on your own, or create a room and share the code with up to three friends.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. On a touchscreen you get a joystick to walk and a big FART button.

Are the farts real recordings?

No. Every fart is generated in code, and each player has their own voice: Classic, Squeaker, Rumbler and Wet One.
