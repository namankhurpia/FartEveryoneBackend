    How to play

    Up to four farters, one round raft. The rim is split evenly between the players (alone, you get all of it) and each can walk anywhere in their slice. Everyone faces the centre, so a fart pushes the raft from your spot straight through its middle: stand at the back to go forward. Turn away from the centre to make it spin. Get to the checkered finish without touching the red spikes.

    A D / ← →Walk left / right round the rim (inside your slice)

    Gas runs out if you hold too long, and refills once you stop. Run it dry and you're out until it's back to 20%. Each new fart starts with a strong "toot". A gamepad works too.
